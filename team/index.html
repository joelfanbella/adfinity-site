Chairman's message

Dear Friends,

Every story I have been part of has begun with someone willing to watch, listen and believe. In a darkened theatre, a living room or a crowded street, people make room for stories in their lives. Over the years, you have given me that time, that trust and that affection. Your belief has been a constant source of inspiration, and it has taught me that the most enduring connections are built not on attention alone, but on trust.

That belief extends beyond the stories we tell. Whenever people choose a brand, they place their confidence in a promise — a promise of quality, value and an experience worth remembering. For a brand, earning that confidence is only the beginning. The real responsibility lies in delivering on it, consistently, at every point of contact.

In today's dynamic marketplace, brands have more ways than ever to reach people. Yet visibility alone does not guarantee connection, and reach does not always translate into relevance. What makes a brand memorable is its ability to be understood, recognised and trusted. The most effective communication does not compete for attention through volume; it earns attention through relevance, clarity and timing.

This is where the physical world of retail offers a remarkable opportunity. A modern shopping mall is more than a destination for shopping. It is a space where people discover, explore, compare and make decisions. In these moments, brands have an opportunity to become part of the experience — reaching consumers in an environment where attention can turn into genuine engagement.

As India's retail landscape evolves, so does the need for media that combines visibility with accountability. Brands need more than a place to display a message. They need well-positioned media, meaningful audience engagement and a clearer understanding of campaign performance. Digital technology has opened new possibilities for making physical media more intelligent, measurable and responsive to the needs of both brands and consumers.

This is the opportunity Adfinity Global Solutions was created to pursue.

We bring together digital innovation and out-of-home media to help brands connect with people in the places where everyday decisions unfold. Through our digital screen network in leading malls across Bengaluru and Kochi, with further expansion ahead, we are building a media ecosystem designed to make brand communication more relevant, engaging and measurable.

Behind every screen is a team that understands the relationship between a brand's ambition and its business objectives. Our media professionals combine domain expertise, strategic thinking and technology to design campaigns with purpose — guided by a commitment to measurable outcomes and long-term value for our partners.

And we are only beginning.

We are preparing to introduce a patent-pending technology platform designed to bring digital intelligence and deeper engagement into the physical shopping environment. It represents an important step towards a future in which the offline world can offer brands new ways to understand, engage and connect with consumers.

At Adfinity, we see this as more than an evolution in media. We see it as an opportunity to rethink how brands and people meet in the real world — with greater relevance, greater intelligence and a stronger sense of purpose.

People often attribute success to luck. I believe opportunity favours those who prepare for it, recognise it and have the conviction to act when it arrives. At Adfinity Global Solutions, we are building the capabilities, partnerships and technology to help brands make the most of what lies ahead.

The possibilities are expanding. The journey continues. And we look forward to shaping what comes next, together.

Welcome to Adfinity Global Solutions.

Warmest regards,

MammoottyChairman

Leadership

The people accountable for the work.

The leadership of Adfinity Global Solutions.

Mammootty

Chairman

A legendary actor of the Indian film industry, Mammootty has won several accolades and awards including the most coveted National Award in 1989, 1993 and 1998. He has acted in films in multiple languages including English, Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil and Telugu and has also received the Padmashri award in 1998 for his contribution to the field of arts.

Besides his illustrious filmography, Mammootty has been actively engaged in various philanthropic activities such as the Pain and Palliative Care Society and Own Your Water, a project that aims to promote awareness for protecting water bodies and providing water to the needy. Being tech savvy, he is also the Goodwill ambassador of Akshaya, an e-literacy project.

V K Ashraf

Director

A well-known and prominent industrialist based in the UAE, V K Ashraf is a powerhouse industrialist with businesses spanning oil exploration, health care and hospitality. He also serves on the board of numerous leading business ventures in the UAE.

An active philanthropist, V K Ashraf involves himself with various charitable activities, one of which is a special school for children affected by autism. VKM Special School has undertaken over 200 surgeries in the last few years, entirely free of cost, and has administered medical management to over 500 children.

Shibu Mathew

Director

An astute business professional, Shibu Mathew has been a part of various leading enterprises in India, including BPL, Airtel, Reliance and Tata, and possesses a commendable performance record in delivering results and leading teams.

In his last corporate assignment, he was part of the Leadership Team at Tata Communications, heading a business division that contributed over 165 million USD per annum to the group.

At Adfinity Global Solutions, Shibu Mathew brings his experience in sales, marketing communication and technology, helping brands refine their marketing and branding strategies.
