Chairman's message

Dear Friends,

What followed was a journey shaped by curiosity, conviction and the willingness to explore the road less travelled. There were ideas that worked, lessons that made us better, and challenges that pushed us to think beyond what we already knew.

Over time, the question that guided us began to evolve. From asking “What else can we sell?” we moved to asking “What else can we build?” That shift in perspective opened new possibilities for Adfinity, taking us from advertising into engineering, manufacturing, stadium systems, entertainment and intelligent environments.

As we built on our experience in advertising, we began to see opportunities beyond the boundaries of our original business. Each new venture brought its own challenges and, with them, the opportunity to develop new capabilities, explore unfamiliar technologies and find better ways to turn ideas into reality.

Advertising taught us the importance of attention, communication and impact. Engineering and manufacturing brought us closer to the systems and structures that make ideas possible. Stadium systems challenged us to bring scale, precision and performance together. Entertainment gave us new ways to connect with audiences, while intelligent environments encouraged us to explore how technology could transform the spaces around us.

Today, Adfinity brings together media, technology, display solutions, entertainment and emerging intelligent systems. Each domain has its own demands, but they are connected by a shared approach: thinking creatively, engineering practically and delivering with purpose. This combination of perspectives and capabilities allows us to take on new challenges with a broader understanding of what is possible.

Possibilities don’t end at the edge of what we know. They begin there. With every idea we explore, every challenge we take on and every capability we build, we move closer to what’s next. And what’s next has no limit.

Looking back, I see a journey of continuous learning, adaptation and growth. Every step has expanded our understanding of what Adfinity can do and strengthened our ability to bring different disciplines together to create meaningful solutions.

As we look ahead, that same spirit of exploration continues to guide us. We remain committed to building on our experience, embracing new technologies and recognising opportunities wherever they emerge. For me, the true value of progress lies in what it enables us to do next for our business, our people and the possibilities we can create together.

Advancing possibilities. Infinitely.

Warmest regards,

MammoottyChairman

Leadership

The people accountable for the work.

The leadership of Adfinity Global Solutions.

Mammootty

Chairman

A legendary actor of the Indian film industry, Mammootty has won several accolades and awards including the most coveted National Award in 1989, 1993 and 1998. He has acted in films in multiple languages including English, Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil and Telugu and has also received the Padmashri award in 1998 for his contribution to the field of arts.

Besides his illustrious filmography, Mammootty has been actively engaged in various philanthropic activities such as the Pain and Palliative Care Society and Own Your Water, a project that aims to promote awareness for protecting water bodies and providing water to the needy. Being tech savvy, he is also the Goodwill ambassador of Akshaya, an e-literacy project.

V K Ashraf

Director

A well-known and prominent industrialist based in the UAE, V K Ashraf is a powerhouse industrialist with businesses spanning oil exploration, health care and hospitality. He also serves on the board of numerous leading business ventures in the UAE.

An active philanthropist, V K Ashraf involves himself with various charitable activities, one of which is a special school for children affected by autism. VKM Special School has undertaken over 200 surgeries in the last few years, entirely free of cost, and has administered medical management to over 500 children.

Shibu Mathew

Director

An astute business professional, Shibu Mathew has been a part of various leading enterprises in India, including BPL, Airtel, Reliance and Tata, and possesses a commendable performance record in delivering results and leading teams.

In his last corporate assignment, he was part of the Leadership Team at Tata Communications, heading a business division that contributed over 165 million USD per annum to the group.

At Adfinity Global Solutions, Shibu Mathew brings his experience in sales, marketing communication and technology, helping brands refine their marketing and branding strategies.
