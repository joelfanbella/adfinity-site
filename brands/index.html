Brand architecture

Why the family works this way.

Adfinity is the parent: the engineering discipline, the client relationships and the accountability sit here. Fanbella and CSIR are horizons with their own audiences and their own identities, because an entertainment community and a responsibility initiative should not sound like a hardware brochure.

FORTIS and REDBUD are engineering platforms. When a specification says FORTIS, it means a known structural and thermal standard rather than a model number.

One brand, multiple horizons — with enough room in the architecture for the next one.
