Six services

What each stage actually includes.

Consulting, design, manufacturing, installation, content and maintenance.

01Consulting & Planning

Before a single panel is specified, we work out what the display is for. Audience, sightlines, dwell time, regulatory limits and the commercial case all shape the answer — and sometimes the answer is a smaller screen in a better place.

02Design & Engineering

Our engineering team designs the display, the structure that holds it and the route a technician takes to service it as one system. That is where most display projects succeed or quietly fail.

03Manufacturing

Cabinets, masks and mounting hardware are produced to the project's geometry rather than adapted from a catalogue part, then burned in and colour-calibrated before anything ships.

04Installation & Commissioning

Installation crews work to a method statement built for the specific site — live retail floors, airside terminals, match-day stadia and public highways all carry different constraints.

05Content & Control

A display is only as good as what plays on it. We set up the control stack, scheduling and data integrations, and we hand the creative team templates authored for the real pixel grid.

06Maintenance & Warranty

Displays are long-life assets, and they behave like it only if someone looks after them. Our service programmes combine remote monitoring, scheduled visits and a held spares pool with response times written into the contract.
