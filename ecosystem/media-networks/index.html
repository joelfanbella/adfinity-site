Ecosystem 01 / Media Networks

Bringing Brands Into the Real World.

Adfinity builds, manages and operates high-visibility Digital Out-of-Home advertising networks that put brands directly in front of active, moving audiences.

Plan a campaign

The proposition

Attention happens outside, in motion.

Digital out-of-home is the one medium an audience cannot scroll past, close or block. It works because it is part of the physical journey people are already making — the commute, the concourse, the walk through a mall.

Adfinity owns the whole chain: we select and secure the sites, engineer and build the displays, run the network operations and report on what actually played. That removes the usual gap between the media company that sells the space and the contractor responsible for whether the screen is working.

One team is accountable for the audience and for the asset.

Network formats

Four environments. One operating standard.

Each format reaches a different audience in a different mood — and each one is planned, built and measured the same rigorous way.

Roadside & Highway

Large-format digital structures on arterial routes and highways, engineered for distance, speed and daylight legibility.

Retail & Mall

Atrium walls, concourse screens and storefront media inside high-dwell retail environments.

Transit & Airport

Terminal, concourse and interchange screens reaching audiences with time on their hands and attention to spare.

Captive dwell · premium audience profile

Premium Office & Hospitality

Lobby and lift-lobby networks in grade-A towers, hotels and business districts.

Repeat daily reach · decision-maker audience

How the network runs

Plan. Build. Operate. Report.

01

Plan

Audience, route and dwell analysis decide where a screen goes — and sometimes decide against one. Site selection is a data exercise before it is a commercial one.

02

Build

Because we engineer and manufacture our own displays, a network site is specified, structurally designed and installed by one accountable team.

03

Operate

Scheduling, campaign management and content operations run from our control stack, with uptime monitored at module level.

04

Report

Proof-of-play, impression delivery and asset health reported per campaign, with programmatic DOOH integration where the client needs it.
