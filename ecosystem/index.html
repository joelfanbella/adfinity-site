Why horizons

Divisions describe a company. Horizons describe a direction.

A division is a box. It tells you what a team is allowed to do and, more quietly, what it is not. We have found that the most interesting work at Adfinity happens in the overlap — when the media team asks the engineering team for something that does not exist yet, or when an entertainment idea needs hardware nobody sells.

So we describe our areas as horizons. Each one is a direction we are moving in, with real capability behind it today, and each one is deliberately close enough to the others to borrow from them.

It also means the list is not closed. Emerging Horizons is a horizon in its own right.
