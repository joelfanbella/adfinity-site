Ecosystem 03 / Fanbella

Where Entertainment Meets Participation.

Fanbella is Adfinity's evolving ecosystem for media and entertainment, bringing together technology, audience passion and storytelling to create new ways for people to discover, participate, express themselves and connect.

Download the appPartner with Fanbella

The idea

Fandom is not an audience metric. It is a behaviour.

Most entertainment is still built on the assumption that people watch. Fanbella starts from the opposite assumption: people want in. They want to predict, argue, create, compete, show up and be recognised for caring.

Fanbella is Adfinity's space for building that — an evolving ecosystem rather than a single product, because the formats that work are still being discovered. Some of it lives on a phone, some of it lives on a 100 square metre screen, and the interesting part is where the two meet.

It is the horizon where our media reach, our display engineering and a genuine appetite for storytelling all point at the same thing.

Four behaviours

Discover. Participate. Express. Connect.

Every Fanbella format has to earn at least one of these. The best ones earn all four.

01

Discover

Formats and feeds built around what an audience is already passionate about, rather than what a schedule says they should watch next.

02

Participate

Voting, predicting, co-creating, competing. The audience has something to do, not just something to watch — in the venue and on the second screen.

03

Express

Creator and community tooling that lets fans make, remix and share — with their work finding its way onto real screens in real places.

04

Connect

The point of all of it: people finding the other people who care about the same thing, in a space that belongs to them.

What is being built

Three tracks, at three different stages.

We would rather be honest about what is live and what is still forming.

  • Live venue participation

    Fanzone screens, crowd prompts and in-venue interaction that turn a stand full of strangers into a single audience — powered by the same display systems we build for stadia.

    In market

  • Second-screen companion formats

    Mobile experiences timed to live moments, so what happens in the venue and what happens in a fan's hand are part of the same show.

    In build

  • Creator and community tooling

    Lightweight tools for fan creators, with a route for their content to reach physical screens across the Adfinity media network.

    Exploring

In the work

Screens, stories, and the second screen.
